# Project Origin

This repository is a portfolio restructuring of an MSc-level academic research project on menstrual-hygiene awareness, product preferences, and switching behavior.

## What was the original work

The original analysis was conducted as part of an M.Sc. Statistics program. It included a survey instrument, data collection, and statistical analysis of awareness, product importance, and switching behavior.

## What changed for this portfolio version

- **Privacy-safe data publishing.** Direct and unnecessarily identifying fields were removed from the public analytical dataset. The original questionnaire data is not included in this repository.
- **Reproducible workflow.** The analysis was restructured into a four-stage pipeline (cleaning, exploratory analysis, awareness modeling, behavioral modeling) with R Markdown files that regenerate all outputs.
- **Statistical rigor documented.** Model diagnostics, proportional-odds considerations, and the reasoning behind simplifying specifications are now explicit.
- **Business-relevant framing.** Statistical results are interpreted as sample associations, not causal effects, with explicit warnings about generalizability.
- **Class-imbalance awareness.** Current product choice was deliberately excluded as a headline classifier because sanitary-pad usage dominated the outcome.

## What stayed the same

The core research questions, the survey data, and the primary statistical approaches (Kruskal-Wallis, logistic regression, ordinal logistic regression) remain as they were in the original work.

## Why this matters

Academic projects often lack the reproducibility, privacy hygiene, and business framing that professional analytics work requires. This portfolio version adds those elements without inflating what the data supports.
